# Install Wookey

Wookey supports macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Download the notarized DMG from the [latest stable release](https://github.com/movvgli/Wookey/releases/latest), open it, and drag Wookey to **Applications**. [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/movvgli/Wookey/releases) also lists preview builds. A preview may have unsigned or unnotarized components that macOS blocks.

## First steps

- Press **Option–Space** to find apps, files, folders, templates, and workflows. Press **Return** to open the selected result or **Command–Return** to search Google in your browser. You can change the search shortcut in **Settings → General**.
- Save frequently used text and images in **Templates**. Create a workflow to open apps, paste templates, wait, or arrange windows in sequence.
- Shake a file or link twice while dragging it from Finder or a browser to open the drawer. Drag the mascot to move the drawer. Adjust shake sensitivity in **Settings → General** and storage duration in **Settings → Retention**.
- Choose whether Wookey opens at login and appears in the Dock or menu bar in **Settings → General**. Choose colors, characters, and icon style separately in **Settings → Theme**.

## Permissions and updates

Accessibility is required for automatic pasting, window arrangement, and the Hyper key. Two-step shortcuts and the Hyper key also use Input Monitoring. You can open the corresponding macOS controls from **Settings → Permissions**. Opening apps or links and copying templates works without those permissions.

The stable app checks for updates daily while running. To check immediately, choose **Check for Updates…** from the Wookey menu. Automatic downloads are off by default.
